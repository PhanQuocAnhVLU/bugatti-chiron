Exterior Design
Sculpted Perfection

Art Meets
Aerodynamics

Every curve of the Chiron's body is shaped by the laws of physics and elevated by the passion of Bugatti's designers. The iconic C-shaped line in polished aluminium defines its silhouette, separating the upper and lower body in perfect proportion.

  • Active Aerodynamics — Adaptive spoiler and diffuser respond in milliseconds to driving conditions
  • Carbon Fibre Monocoque — Ultra-lightweight yet incredibly rigid passenger cell
  • Bespoke Colour Program — Over 8,000 colour combinations crafted to each owner's vision
  • Polished Aluminium Arch — The signature C-line, individually machined from a solid block
  • Matrix LED Lighting — Quad circular headlights and horseshoe rear light signature
The Driver's Sanctuary

A Cockpit of
Pure Luxury

Step inside and every detail speaks of artisanal mastery. Fine leather hand-stitched by craftsmen, exposed carbon fibre, polished metal, and a digital cockpit that places the driver's needs above all else.

  • Hand-Stitched Leather — Full-grain leather with contrast stitching in exclusive colour choices
  • Digital Instrument Cluster — High-resolution circular display with analogue speedometer
  • Premium Sound System — Bespoke audio system tuned for acoustic perfection
Interior Design
W16
The Heart of the Beast

8.0L Quad-Turbo
W16 Engine

The Chiron's heart is a hand-assembled 8.0-litre W16 engine with four turbochargers operating in two stages. At low revs, two turbos provide immediate response. At high revs, all four ignite with ferocious force.
